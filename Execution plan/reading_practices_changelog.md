## 2026-07-30 — Goal-fitness pass (diagnostic: goal alignment 3/5, feedback robustness 2/5, contribution pipeline 2/5, cross-document consistency 2/5)

Prompted by a diagnostic review scoring the document on nine dimensions against the Phase III publication target. The receptive half scored 4/5 and is untouched; every change below is on the productive side or in the consistency layer.

- **Light tier resolved to per chapter / major section, 150–250 w** (was "per text, 200–300 w" in Practice A, "per chapter, 150–250 w" everywhere else including the execution plan's Appendix A mirror and its own week rows). The hour formula settles it: `n_chapters` is assigned per text irrespective of tier and the Light writing cap is `n_chapters × 0.75h`, so per-chapter is what the budgets always priced. Six locations aligned; the degradation-ladder note (~150 w) restated as the bottom of the Light band.
- **The candidate register** — new section at the head of Part II. Adopts the existing vault artifact (`Ideen/` + `tpl-research-idea.md`) rather than inventing one; adds the method side: what feeds it (six seed-generating practices), the recurrence threshold for opening an entry, and the harvest cadence (R2, R3, M32 Phase II review, R5). Closes the gap in which 35 months of closing sentences, intervention points, and negative-space notes had no ledger and R5's three-candidate exercise ran from memory.
- **The positioning check** — a four-step, ~90-min kill test (forward-citation sweep · venue sweep · PhilPapers/*SEP* browse · one human), defined inside the register section and operationalising the template's existing *Kill criteria* field. Runs from Skizze #3 (~M29); hard gate before the M38 W3.2 topic lock-in. Answers the gap Practice J had named and left open.
- **External calibration extended past Phase I** — three touchpoints became five, and the section was renamed from "the Phase I tutor." Two move onto the productive ladder (Skizze #3 ~M29, *Zwischenstück* draft ~M34), where the previous design left no external reading at all until M41–M43. Two errors corrected: touchpoint 2 was anchored to "~M6, first Habermas reconstruction," which is unschedulable (TKH is ~M18) — it is now the first *Kapital* Full at ~W23–24, a better check anyway; and touchpoint 3 was described as folded into the W59–61 external-calibration letter, which is a cold-contact question sent with no attachment — it is now a separate send of the Field Report.
- **Claude Use 1 promoted to standing in Phase I** (was first-of-author only), tapering to first-of-author from Phase II. Rationale from the document's own evidence base: the retrieval-practice results underwriting the protocols are results about tested retrieval *with corrective feedback*; Phase I was otherwise a 15-month closed loop. Cost ~10 min per Full; hallucination discipline restated for the higher frequency; `/recon-check` named as the operationalisation.
- **Comprehension check restructured** from an untimed ~2–3h written self-test to **45 min closed-book + 45 min gap repair (~1.5h)**. The check asks *do I have the apparatus?*, which is only answerable under retrieval conditions; the visible gap between the two passes is the module's most honest diagnostic. Frees ~1h per close (~12–15h programme-wide), which is what the calibrator touchpoints and positioning checks consume. Module-close total ~5–6.5h → ~4–5h.
- **`[prem:X]` made operative from Module 2**, not held behind the reinstatement rule. Practice A calls premise mis-attribution the commonest error on immanent-critique texts, and those start at Marx — the old rule meant learning the tag by failing on the text where it matters most.
- **Beginner-mode ↔ W13 gate collision resolved.** "Budget the slow row" was unactionable (the plan's weekly page targets are bottom-of-range and not the reader's to re-budget) and collided with the calibration gate, which measures exactly the scaffold texts and reads slow pace as a pacing problem. Replaced with: expect mid-to-slow, do not compensate by reading faster, annotate the log `first-scaffold`, let the gate price it. Also corrected the illustration, which cited the Transparent **DE** row for Neuhouser (scheduled in English).

Cross-document: `execution_plan.md` §B gains standing entries for the calibrator and the register, with inline rows at R1/W12 (recruit), W16–17, W23–24, W61, M29, M32, M34, R5 and the M38 lock-in gate; §D.1 carries the Use-1 change and §D.2 the comprehension-check restructure. `programme_parameters.md` §3.3 gains (d) External calibration and restates (a) at ~1.5h. Per-week close-week hour figures and module tallies still carry the old ~5.5–6h close and are flagged as conservative by ~1h pending the next full recompile.
